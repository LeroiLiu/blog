<h2>1、InfluxDB安装</h2>
<h4>1.1、下载&#xff1a;</h4>
<p><strong>网址&#xff1a;</strong><a href="https://portal.influxdata.com/downloads/" rel="nofollow">https://portal.influxdata.com/downloads/</a></p>
<p><img alt="" height="440" src="/images/blog/87255147/local-001.png" width="618" /></p>
<h4>1.2、安装&#xff1a;</h4>
<p><strong>示例&#xff1a;</strong>以centOS为例&#xff0c;按操作步骤进行即可</p>
<div class="language-shell vp-adaptive-theme"><button title="Copy Code" class="copy"></button><span class="lang">shell</span><pre class="vp-code" tabindex="0" v-pre><code>wget https://dl.influxdata.com/influxdb/releases/influxdb-1.7.4.x86_64.rpm
sudo yum localinstall influxdb-1.7.4.x86_64.rpm</code></pre></div>
<p><img alt="" height="151" src="/images/blog/87255147/local-002.png" width="855" /></p>
<p class="article-note"><span>注意&#xff1a;如果之前有安装过其他版本的influx&#xff0c;超链可能会失效&#xff0c;出现错误。重新设置即可。</span></p>
